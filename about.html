About us

Every breed, every background, every goal

Whether you’re dealing with disobedience, leash pulling, or more serious issues like aggression or anxiety, we’re ready to help.

Our team of professional trainers works with dogs of all breeds, sizes, and behavioral backgrounds. From basic manners to off-leash control, we design each program around your dog’s needs — and your goals as an owner.

We start with a free consultation so you can meet our team, see what’s possible, and ask any questions. Financing options are available and can be discussed during your consultation or by giving us a call.

How we train

Four things we hold to

01

Clarity before correction

A dog can't be held to a standard they were never taught. We teach the behavior thoroughly, in stages, before we ever expect it under pressure.

02

The relationship is the tool

Trust is what makes a dog choose you over a squirrel. We build engagement and confidence first — obedience follows a dog who actually wants to work with you.

03

Train the whole household

If one person's rules differ from everyone else's, the dog learns the loophole. Family members are welcome — and encouraged — at sessions.

04

Honest expectations

Some things take six weeks and some take six months. We'd rather tell you that at the free evaluation than sell you a timeline that was never real.

Teddy Wall · Owner & head trainer

Meet the owner

Teddy Wall

Originally from Long Island, NY, I’ve called Murfreesboro home since 1996 and earned my degree in criminal justice from MTSU in 2001.

A lifelong athlete and avid CrossFitter, I’ve always had a deep love for animals — something I now share with my amazing daughter.

After years in the corporate world, I followed my passion and made the leap into professional dog training. I trained under several experienced professionals and attended a school dedicated to canine instruction before opening my own training business.

With a background working with everything from protection dogs to family pets, I’ve developed a true passion for helping dogs become well-behaved, confident companions. There’s nothing more rewarding than seeing a family thrive with a happy, obedient dog. I’m proud to bring Talking Tails to Middle Tennessee and look forward to making a difference in the lives of dogs and their people.
