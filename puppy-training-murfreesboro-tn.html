The curriculumWhat your
puppy learns

Puppy sessions are short, gentle and upbeat — built around a young dog's attention span, not an adult dog's curriculum. We teach the handful of things that make the first year easy.

  • House and crate manners, plus a potty-training routine that fits your actual schedule
  • Name recognition, attention and the beginnings of a reliable recall
  • Loose-leash walking, taught before your puppy is strong enough to pull
  • Bite inhibition and an end to needle-sharp nipping on hands, sleeves and ankles
  • Confident socialization — controlled exposure to people, dogs, surfaces and noise, not a free-for-all
  • Handling and grooming tolerance, so the vet visit isn't a wrestling match
  • Settling on cue, so a busy house doesn't mean a frantic puppy

Best for puppies roughly 8 weeks to 6 months. Older than that? The obedience program is the better starting point — and it works just as well.

Puppy training FAQ

Before you call

Anything not covered here, ask at the evaluation — that's what it's for.

How old does my puppy need to be to start training in Murfreesboro?

As soon as your puppy is home and your vet has cleared them — usually around 8 weeks. Waiting until six months means unlearning habits instead of installing them, so earlier is always easier.

Does my puppy need all their shots before the first session?

No. We work around your puppy's vaccination schedule, starting in low-risk environments such as your own home and yard, and expanding to busier Murfreesboro locations as your vet gives the all clear.

How long does puppy training take?

Most families see meaningful change in the first two or three sessions — usually in potty routine, nipping and leash pressure. Reliable, distraction-proof behavior develops over the following weeks and depends heavily on the practice you do between sessions.

Can you help with potty training and crate training?

Yes. Potty training and crate training are part of every puppy program. We build a realistic schedule around your household's actual routine rather than an ideal one nobody can keep.

How do I stop my puppy from biting and nipping?

Bite inhibition is taught, not waited out. We show you how to redirect the behavior, when to end play, and how to teach your puppy that teeth on skin stops the fun — consistently, so it sticks.

What does puppy training cost in Murfreesboro?

It depends on your puppy and the program that fits, which is why we quote after the free evaluation rather than before. Flexible financing is available and we're happy to walk you through it.

Do I need to be there for the sessions?

Yes, and so should everyone else who lives with the puppy. Your puppy will learn the skills either way; the point of training is that you can use them.

Which areas do you serve?

Murfreesboro and the surrounding Rutherford County communities, including Smyrna, La Vergne, Christiana, Rockvale, Eagleville, Blackman and Walter Hill.
