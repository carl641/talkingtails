Good to know

Membership Cancellation Policy

Members must provide at least 30 days' notice to cancel their Talking Tails Membership. Cancellation requests must be submitted by contacting Talking Tails Professional Dog Training directly via email or text (info@talkingtailstn.com or (629) 772-3647).

Upon receiving the cancellation request, the membership will remain active until the end of the current paid billing period. No additional recurring payments will be charged after the cancellation takes effect.

Members may continue to participate in weekly group classes and access available membership support until the end of their paid membership period, provided their account remains in good standing.

For questions or assistance with cancellation, please contact Talking Tails Professional Dog Training directly.
