What to expect

Your first hour with us

The evaluation isn't a sales pitch with a dog in the room. It's the first piece of real information we get about your situation.

We watch your dog be your dog

How they greet a stranger, how they move on the leash, how quickly they settle. Ten minutes of honest behavior tells us more than any questionnaire.

You tell us the real problem

Not the polite version. The 6am barking, the walk you've stopped taking, the guests you've stopped inviting over.

We show you something

A small piece of training, right there, so you can see how your dog responds and what the process actually feels like.

You get a plan and a number

Recommended program, realistic timeline, exact cost, financing options if you want them. Then you go home and think about it.

Ready when you are.

Most people call after one bad walk too many. That's a perfectly good reason to start.
