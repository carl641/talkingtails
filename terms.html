1. This agreement

These terms cover your use of talkingtailstn.com and the dog training services provided by Talking Tails Dog Training (“Talking Tails”, “we”, “us”), based in Murfreesboro, Tennessee. By using this site, submitting a form, or booking training with us, you agree to them.

If we give you a separate written training agreement, board-and-train contract or release form to sign, that document controls wherever it differs from this page.

2. Using this site

Everything here is offered for general information about our training programs. It is not behavioral, veterinary or legal advice for your particular dog, and reading it does not create a client relationship — that starts when we agree on a program together.

We work to keep the site accurate, but details like pricing, class dates and availability change. Nothing on the site is a binding offer until we confirm it with you directly.

Please don't attempt to disrupt the site, scrape it wholesale, or use it to send anything unlawful, threatening or deceptive.

3. Services & quotes

We offer puppy training, obedience training, private lessons, group classes, board-and-train and behavior work. Which program suits your dog is decided at the free evaluation, not before it.

The evaluation is free and carries no obligation. Any price we quote is based on what we saw and what you told us at that evaluation; if the situation turns out to be materially different, we'll talk to you before anything changes.

We can decline or end a program — for example, if a dog presents a danger we're not equipped to manage safely, if a required vaccination is missing, or if instructions are not being followed at home. Where we end a program early for reasons outside your control, we'll refund the unused portion.

4. Booking & payment

Programs are booked with a deposit, with the balance due on the schedule we set out in writing when you enroll. Your spot is held once the deposit clears.

We accept the payment methods listed at the time of booking, and we offer flexible financing through All Pet Card. Financing is a separate agreement between you and the lender; its terms, approval and interest are theirs, not ours, and we're not a party to it.

Sessions that go unpaid past their due date may be paused until the account is current.

5. Scheduling & cancellations

Training runs on appointments, so a missed one is time we can't give to another dog. Please give us at least 24 hours' notice to reschedule a private lesson or evaluation.

We'll do the same for you. If we have to move a session — weather, illness, a trainer's emergency — we'll reach you as early as we can and reschedule at no cost.

Group class fees cover the block of classes, not individual sessions; missed classes generally can't be credited, though we'll help you make up material where we can.

6. Health, vaccination & safety

Every dog who trains with us must be up to date on core vaccinations and free of contagious illness, and we may ask for proof from your veterinarian before the first session.

Please keep a sick or recently-exposed dog at home, and tell us about any bite history, injury, medication, allergy or medical condition that could affect training. We need to know, and a dog is safer when we do.

If a dog is injured or falls ill in our care, we'll try to reach you immediately. If we can't and the situation is urgent, you authorize us to obtain veterinary treatment at your expense.

7. What we need from you

Training is a two-sided job. You agree to:

  • Practise between sessions and follow the plan we give you at home — this is where most of the progress actually happens.
  • Give us accurate information about your dog's history and behavior.
  • Keep your dog leashed and under control around our facilities, class locations and other clients.
  • Carry any insurance or licensing your municipality requires, and comply with local leash and nuisance ordinances.
  • Be the dog's legal owner, or have the owner's permission to enroll them.

8. Results

We're confident in our methods and our trainers, and we're straight with you about what they can do. Dogs are living animals, not appliances: outcomes depend on the dog, the history, the household and how consistently the plan is followed after we leave. We do not guarantee specific results, timelines, or that any behavior will be permanently eliminated.

Any timeline we give you is our honest estimate from experience, not a promise.

You remain responsible for your dog's behavior and for any injury or damage they cause to people, animals or property, except where it is caused by our own negligence.

To the fullest extent Tennessee law allows, our total liability arising out of your use of this site or our services is limited to the amount you paid us for the services in question, and we are not liable for indirect or consequential losses. Nothing here limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

Unless you tell us otherwise, you permit us to use images of your dog this way, without payment. Tell any trainer at any time if you'd rather we didn't, and we'll stop — it changes nothing about your training.
