Board & train in Murfreesboro

How It Works

Our 3 or 5-Day Board & Train gives your dog an immersive head start. Your dog stays with us for three or five days while our trainers do the bulk of the teaching, and then have you ready to start with group class to have fun and train along with others.

Three or five days with our trainers

The board and trains are done Monday through Friday. Your dog trains with our team during the day and goes home each night with our owner, Teddy Wall. Over that time, our trainers build a strong foundation by teaching your dog the fundamentals, with real-world distractions built in.

Go-home lesson

At the end of the last day, you'll get hands-on instruction from your trainer in a go-home lesson. We walk you through everything your dog learned during the week and teach you how to recreate the same communication your dog learned with our trainers.

Lifetime support and group classes

Every Board & Train program comes with lifetime support (including private lessons if needed) and group classes, so your training never has to end. You'll be able to join our group classes when it is convenient for you so you and your dog can keep building new skills for as long as you want.

Our approach

Board & Train With Us

With our 3 or 5-Day Board & Train, most of the teaching happens here with our team. Because your dog is with us for the entire three or five days, our trainers can dedicate that time to building the fundamentals, with real-world distractions built in throughout, including new people, other dogs, sounds, and changing environments. At night, your dog goes home with Teddy, so the same structure carries through into real home life, not just a kennel.

Our trainers teach your dog through touch, movement, and body language, the way dogs naturally communicate. We don't wait for a dog to make a mistake and then correct it. Instead, we make the answer clear from the very start, going out of our way to help your dog get it right the first time. If it doesn't click right away, we adjust how we communicate with that individual dog until it understands what we're asking.

Above all, we focus on the fundamentals. Everything we do starts with making your dog aware of us, because we can't solve a problem if the dog isn't aware of us in the first place. That's why we don't treat behavior problems as separate projects. Jumping, leash pulling, and other issues are resolved through the same fundamentals we begin teaching on day one.

Unlike our Day Train Program, there's no turnover lesson at the end of each day. Your dog's time with us is dedicated to learning, and your part begins with the go-home lesson at the end of the last day. Then you move right into group class to practice those skills. Don't worry: if you are still struggling, you are always welcome to schedule a private lesson to get individual help for the life of your dog.

Is it the right fit?

Who It's For

Our 3 or 5-Day Board & Train is ideal if you want an immersive jumpstart, if your schedule makes daily drop-offs and pickups difficult, or if you're heading out of town and want your dog's time away to be productive. It works for young dogs starting fresh and older dogs who picked up habits along the way. Because your dog comes home already understanding the fundamentals, your transition into group class is really easy.

It's also important to know what this program isn't. Our 3 or 5-Day Board & Train is not a drop-off-and-done service for owners who want a trained dog without being involved afterward. Every dog training program requires owner input to maintain and grow what the dog has learned, and ours is no exception. Our trainers lay the foundation, but your consistency is what keeps it strong and helps it grow.

The one exception is dogs with an extensive bite history. For those dogs, we start with private lessons only, so you and your trainer can work together one-on-one from the very beginning.
