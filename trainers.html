Teddy Wall

Owner / Head Trainer

Hi, I’m the owner at Talking Tails Dog Training. Long Island, NY native and MTSU criminal-justice grad who traded the corporate world for a career in canine training after realizing my unmatched passion for dogs.

Read more

I’ve called Murfreesboro home since 1996 and earned my degree in criminal justice from MTSU in 2001. A lifelong athlete and avid CrossFitter, I’ve always had a deep love for animals — something I now share with my amazing daughter.

After years in the corporate world, I followed my passion and made the leap into professional dog training. I trained under several experienced professionals and attended a school dedicated to canine instruction before opening Talking Tails.

With a background working with everything from protection dogs to family pets, I’ve developed a true passion for helping dogs become well-behaved, confident companions. There’s nothing more rewarding than seeing a family thrive with a happy, obedient dog.

Susie Tramel

Sales

Hey there, I handle sales at Talking Tails Dog Training. I grew up on my family’s farm in Pall Mall, TN, earned my Horticulture degree from MTSU, and joined Talking Tails to keep making people (and their pups) smile.

Read more

Growing up on a farm in Pall Mall meant animals were never a hobby — they were the day. That’s where I learned patience, consistency and how much easier life gets when an animal understands what you’re asking.

These days I’m the one who walks you through your options, answers the “is this really going to work for my dog?” questions honestly, and makes sure the program you choose actually fits your family and your budget.

I was also featured on Good Morning Murfreesboro, where I got to share our approach and how we help build strong, confident relationships between dogs and their owners.

Alyssa Villanueva

Office Manager

Mabuhay! I’m the Office Manager at Talking Tails Dog Training. Originally from the Philippines with a Marketing degree, I’m usually the first person you’ll speak with when you call.

I keep the schedule straight, the paperwork painless and the phone answered — so when you call about your dog, you get a real person who can actually help.

My marketing background means I spend a lot of time on the other side of the conversation: making sure owners know what to expect before, during and after training, and that nobody is left guessing about next steps.
